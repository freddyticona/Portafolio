DATO (citado en el artículo 1529):
"The Nova 7X and Nova 7X Gen 2 headset connects wirelessly through the included 2.4 GHz dongle."
"Plug the dongle into any USB port on your Xbox using the included USB-A cable. Make sure the dongle switch is set to 'Xbox' mode. The same dongle can be switched from 'Xbox' to 'USB', allowing you to use your headset on: PC, PlayStation, Nintendo Switch, Android devices, Meta Quest."

Tabla comparativa de la segunda fuente:
"Xbox Compatible | Nova 7: ❌ | Nova 7P: ❌ | Nova 7X: ✅"

Conclusión verificada: para conexión inalámbrica con Xbox se requiere la variante 7X (o 7X Gen 2); Nova 7 y Nova 7P no son compatibles con Xbox de forma inalámbrica.
